This educational guide is designed to encourage self-awareness and gentle stress relief. It does not replace individualized clinical care. If you are experiencing symptoms that impede daily living, consider reaching out to a licensed counselor or physician.

Self-Inquiry

Post-Reading Reflection Prompts

Take two minutes to let what you read settle into your thoughts. Tap any prompt to explore:

Notice your jaw, the skin around your eyes, your shoulders, or your belly. Try taking one slow breath and consciously softening that area by just 10%.
It could be delaying a non-urgent email response by 30 minutes, stepping outside for 60 seconds without a screen, or saying "let me think about that" before automatically agreeing.
If an inner critic is demanding perfection, imagine offering the same kind, quiet understanding you would naturally extend to a dear friend.
