Session Arc

What Happens During a 50-Minute Consultation

Knowing the structure beforehand helps alleviate anticipatory anxiety. Here is how our sample professionals hold space:

1. Gentle Arrival (0-10m)

Settling in with a brief grounding breath, testing audio/video ease, and establishing comfort.

2. Context & Needs (10-30m)

Sharing what has felt heavy lately at your own natural pace without pressure to overshare.

3. Tool Exploration (30-45m)

Practicing a customized somatic or cognitive technique tailored to your exact situation.

4. Grounded Close (45-50m)

Reflecting on key takeaways and deciding if you wish to meet again—completely zero pressure.

🛡️ Your Complete Autonomy as a Client

In any legitimate counseling relationship, you remain the expert of your own lived experience. You have the unconditional right to:

  • Say "I'd rather not talk about that today" at any moment.
  • Request a 60-second silence or water pause whenever feeling flooded.
  • Switch to a different practitioner whose cultural background or communication style better fits your heart.
