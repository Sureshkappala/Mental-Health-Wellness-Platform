Immediate Crisis Hotlines

If you are feeling overwhelmed, hopeless, or in immediate danger of self-harm, please do not wait for an email response. Verified 24/7 confidential crisis services:

  • USA & Canada: Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
  • United Kingdom: Call 111 or Samaritans at 116 123
  • International: Contact your nearest hospital emergency department or emergency services.
