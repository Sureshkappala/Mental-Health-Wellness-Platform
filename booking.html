Practical Tips

Online Consultation Comfort Checklist

Make the most of your virtual session with these 4 grounding preparation steps:

🎧 Private Audio

Wearing headphones prevents echo, protects your personal privacy from household members, and enhances voice clarity.

☕ Sensory Comfort

Keep a glass of water, soothing herbal tea, and a soft blanket nearby to support bodily regulation during emotional moments.

📝 Optional Notes

Jot down 2 or 3 bullet points in advance so you don't worry about forgetting important feelings when the session begins.

🕊️ 5-Minute Buffer

Avoid booking back-to-back work meetings. Give yourself 5 quiet minutes before and after your consultation to breathe.
