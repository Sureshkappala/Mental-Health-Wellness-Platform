Credential Clarity

Understanding Practitioner Roles

Navigating mental health titles can feel confusing. Here is a clear, compassionate guide to finding the right match for your situation:

🧠 Clinical Psychologist (PsyD / PhD)

Specializes in in-depth behavioral assessments, cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), and evidence-based neuro-regulation for chronic stress or anxiety patterns.

🤝 Marriage & Family Therapist (LMFT)

Focuses on relational dynamics, communication breakdowns, boundary conflicts, and navigating major family or career life transitions.

🌿 Somatic & Clinical Social Worker (LCSW / MSW)

Emphasizes holistic person-in-environment care, nervous system relaxation, sleep restructuring, and community wellness resources.

Consultation Prep

Gentle Preparation for Your First Session

Meeting a therapist or counselor for the first time is a collaborative exploration, not a high-stakes interview. Keep these comfortable principles in mind:

✓
You set the pace: You never have to disclose traumatic experiences or painful memories before you feel completely ready and safe.
✓
Ask about their philosophy: Ask how they approach boundary setting, mindfulness, or somatic work to see if their style resonates with you.
✓
Comfortable space: For online video sessions, bring a glass of water, wear comfortable clothes, and sit in a private, quiet corner.
