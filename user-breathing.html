🌿 How Diaphragmatic Breathing Works

When exhales are intentionally longer than inhales, your heart rate slows and your vagus nerve sends safety signals directly to your brainstem. If at any moment you feel dizzy or breathless, return immediately to your normal natural breathing pattern.
