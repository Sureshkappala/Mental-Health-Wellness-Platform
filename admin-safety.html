Platform Operating Scope

🔒 Complete Isolation of Personal Reflections

MindBloom deliberately restricts administrative accounts from querying, exporting, or viewing user private journal entries or individual mood notes. Personal reflection content belongs solely to the individual and resides strictly in their device’s private local storage.

🌱 Non-Clinical, Educational Boundaries

This platform does not diagnose psychiatric conditions, prescribe pharmaceutical treatments, or provide clinical triage. Every article, tool, and breathing exercise is designed as educational grounding support for everyday stress and self-paced mindfulness.

🚨 Crisis Protocol Architecture

In any scenario where an individual reports thoughts of self-harm, severe clinical depression, or acute emergency crisis, the interface immediately surfaces verified 24/7 emergency contacts (such as 988 Lifeline, 111 NHS, and local hospital triage).
